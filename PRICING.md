# Pricing strategy

Draft. Paid pricing and AI allowances are TBD based on costs.

## Free

- Up to 10 saved recipes.
- A small lifetime allowance for AI imports and recipe generation.
- Manual entry, editing, scaling, cooking mode, basic shopping lists, and individual recipe exports.

## Paid — $X/month

- Unlimited saved recipes, or a generous limit to be decided.
- A monthly allowance for AI imports and recipe generation.
- Create and manage a shared household, if ready for launch. Free users can join.

## Possible paid features later

- Multi-week meal planning and reusable meal plans.
- Unlimited collections and bulk organisation.
- Bulk exports and printable recipe books.
- Extra AI credit purchases.

These are proposed features and tier boundaries, not a list of existing capabilities.

## Billing and cost controls

- Keep Better Auth and use Polar for billing.
- Offer a short free trial with a small total AI allowance.
- Offer friends-and-family coupons, including free access where appropriate.
- Apply AI limits to every account, including trials and discounted subscriptions.
- Track saved recipes separately from AI usage. Deleting a recipe does not restore AI credits.
- Enforce usage limits on the server and add an application-wide spending cutoff.

## Downgrades

- Keep existing recipes readable and exportable.
- Block new recipes while the account is at or above the free limit of 10.
- Return AI access to the remaining free allowance; do not reset it on cancellation.

## Launch message

Keep your first 10 recipes free. Upgrade to grow your cookbook and import more.
